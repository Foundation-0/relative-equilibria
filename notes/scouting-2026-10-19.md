# Scouting notes, 19 October 2026

This is the research behind the repository, from Foundation0's
[scouting of 18–19 October 2026](https://github.com/Foundation-0/f0/tree/main/deep-fields). It is split by how far
each fact was checked. Check a fact before you build on it.

## The project

- **Question.** Exactly how many equilibrium shapes (central configurations) do 8 equal masses have, and how many
  relative equilibria do 5, 6 and 7 identical point vortices have? Answered with computer-certified counts.
- **Hub.** [physics0](https://github.com/Foundation-0/physics0); also of interest to
  [math0](https://github.com/Foundation-0/math0).
- **Sibling project.** [point-charges](https://github.com/Foundation-0/point-charges), whose interval-arithmetic
  certifier this project reuses. Interval arithmetic computes with ranges that are guaranteed to contain the true
  values. The files are `certify.py`, `ia.py`, `make_certs.py`, `verify_arb.py` and `tests/`. Read
  point-charges' README and NOTE.md first.
- **Status.** Not started. The lead is to be named.

**Plain explanation.** A central configuration is an arrangement of masses in which each body's gravitational pull
points at the centre of mass, in proportion to its distance from it. Spun at the right speed, the arrangement keeps
its shape forever. Point vortices in a fluid have the same kind of rigidly rotating arrangements, with a
logarithmic interaction instead of gravity. Counting them is a classical problem.

## Checked on 19 Oct 2026

- Moczurad and Zgliczyński, [arXiv:1812.07279](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.07279): a "computer assisted proof of
  the full listing of central configuration ... for n = 5, 6, 7 with equal masses".
  - n = 5 took 24 s, n = 6 about 1 h, n = 7 almost 100 h.
  - "For this reason we were not able to obtain a rigorous listing of CCs for n = 8".
  - The paper does prove that non-symmetric central configurations exist for n = 8, 9 and 10.

## From the scouting report, not yet re-checked

- **Finiteness.** Whether the count is always finite is Smale's sixth problem. It is proved for 4 bodies (Hampton
  and Moeckel 2006) and for 5 bodies for almost all masses (Albouy and Kaloshin 2012).
- **Unequal masses.** Moczurad and Zgliczyński moved to unequal masses for five bodies
  ([arXiv:2601.01165](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01165), January and July 2026).
- **Identical vortices.**
  - A September 2026 paper classifies only N = 4 ([arXiv:2609.15090](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15090)).
  - For N ≥ 5 there are only numerical catalogues: Campbell and Ziff 1978, and numerical continuation (following each
    solution as a parameter changes) for N ≤ 8 in a
    [2012 thesis](https://ross.aoe.vt.edu/papers/dirksen-2012-thesis.pdf).
  - Rigorous results so far cover only some configurations on the sphere
    ([arXiv:2309.04320](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.04320)).
  - Identical vortices might have continuous families of equilibria rather than a finite count. That would itself
    be a result.
- **Four bodies.**
  - Four-body cases are busy: [arXiv:2606.19596](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19596),
    [arXiv:2609.01857](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01857), [arXiv:2610.04593](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04593). So
    this project starts at 5 vortices and 8 masses.
  - Tomar settled four-body convex uniqueness for all masses with interval arithmetic plus Lean (a proof checker)
    on 28 Sep 2026 ([arXiv:2609.35632](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35632)). Convex means that no body lies inside
    the triangle of the other three.
- **Cost of 8 masses.** The authors (Moczurad and Zgliczyński) estimate about 4 × 10^4 times more boxes (small pieces
  of the search space) per extra body, so n = 8 needs a much better method: symmetry reduction, or homotopy continuation (a numerical method that
  finds all solutions of a system of polynomial equations) to find candidates, followed by certification.
- **Tools.** IntervalArithmetic.jl, HomotopyContinuation.jl, the CAPD library, and point-charges' Python interval
  code and Arb-based verifier.
